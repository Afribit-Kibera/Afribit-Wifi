# M-PESA payment mark

`mpesa.svg` is the unchanged SVG served by Safaricom's official developer
portal at <https://developer.safaricom.co.ke/static/mpesa-logo.svg>, downloaded
5 October 2026. The published Safaricom logo download page is
<https://www.safaricom.co.ke/brand-assets?id=2021>; that page returned HTTP 403
in this environment, so the official developer portal asset was used.

The mark identifies the M-PESA payment method; it is not the Mesh brand or an
endorsement. Preserve its colours, proportions and surrounding space. M-PESA
and the logo remain their owners' trademarks. Public availability of this
asset does not grant ownership or blanket permission for other brand uses.
The SVG is served locally so captive customers do not need to reach an
external image host.
