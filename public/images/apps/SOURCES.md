# Official app marks

Downloaded on 6 October 2026; SVGs are unmodified apart from proportional display sizing. They identify the independent apps, not an endorsement of Mesh.

- Blink square icon: https://www.blink.sv/en/brand — original asset https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6720ed07d56bdfa402a08023/6720ed07d56bdfa402a081b9_Blink%20logo_icon.svg
- Signal ultramarine logo: https://signal.org/brand/ — official bundle https://signal.org/brand/bundles/Signal%20Logo.zip, `Logo/SVG/Signal-Logo-Ultramarine.svg`.

Copies in `mikrotik/hotspot-mesh/` are served locally, so rendering these marks does not require a third-party request. Signal is displayed at 36 pixels with 16 pixels of space around the mark.
