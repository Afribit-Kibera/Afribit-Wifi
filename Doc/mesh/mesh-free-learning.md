# Free learning and app entry

6 October 2026, Primary home pilot.

The router welcome displays free learning and installed Signal/Blink
information with locally served official logos. The former **Use Mesh without
internet** redirect link was removed at the operator's request on 6 October:
opening another webpage did not dismiss the captive window. `mesh.html`
remains manually accessible with the purchase dock. Free access does not
create a paid session or bypass HotSpot.

Its cover identifies My First Bitcoin, Bitcoin Diploma, Student Workbook,
English Version 2025. The public link goes to the publisher's unchanged PDF;
the 23 MB document is not uploaded to the router or included in the website

identity intact. This is not an Afribit-authored workbook or an assertion of

photo provenance is recorded in [art direction](mesh-captive-art-direction.md).

photo-candidates directory. They illustrate the community, not Bitcoin Kenya

assets with Sharp; it does not generate or alter their scene. New derivatives
total approximately **118 KB**. All card imagery, Geist and the Bitcoin mark
are served locally on Primary; external sites/PDF need the network uplink.

## Network allowance and acceptance

The pinned learning-site commissioner now includes `bitcoinekasi.com` and its
`www` variant alongside the three existing learning domains. Rules are guest
IPv4, TCP 443 only; no paid grant or general-internet bypass is created. The
original PDF's origin is reachable within that allowance. Unpaid phone loading
of the complete PDF remains a physical acceptance step.

Destination-IP allowances cannot strictly isolate hostnames on shared hosting.
Other subdomains, third-party video/CDN assets, store downloads and linked sites
are not automatically free. The initial learning sites and publisher allowance
are a bounded home trial; field IPv6 isolation and strict domain policy remain
production gates.

Signal text was accepted earlier by both phones while unrelated browsing was
blocked. On this update, the operator confirmed an installed, signed-in **Blink
wallet refresh works on unpaid Mesh, cellular data off**. This does not test
wallet sends, installation, new login, every feature or offline delivery. See
[Blink allowance and source research](mesh-blink-free-access.md).

Eight static router files were backed up, installed and read back byte-for-byte
at 15:31 EAT. Site configuration, DHCP and existing paid policy were preserved.
Private backup: `artifacts/mesh-lab/private/persistent-portal/20261006T123151Z/`.
The free-learning firewall additions were separately backed up by their scoped
commissioner. No live payment or voucher issuance was performed.
