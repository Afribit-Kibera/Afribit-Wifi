What is Mesh?

Mesh is community Wi-Fi by Afribit. Connect to explore the free learning websites, or buy an internet pass for everything else. Nearby access points help more people connect. As local services are added, you'll be able to use them here too.

Mesh is the network. A pass gives this device access to the wider internet. You don't need a Bitcoin wallet to get started.

How do I get internet?

Choose a pass, enter your M-Pesa number, then approve the payment on your phone. Stay connected to Mesh. Your internet turns on automatically when your payment is confirmed.

Why Bitcoin?

Bitcoin is an open network for sending and receiving money without a bank account. Learn how wallets work and how to use them safely with our free resources. You can still buy an internet pass with M-Pesa; no Bitcoin wallet needed.

Can I buy internet later?

Yes. Stay connected to Mesh and open your Mesh page in Safari or Chrome. Save it so you can return after closing this welcome window.
